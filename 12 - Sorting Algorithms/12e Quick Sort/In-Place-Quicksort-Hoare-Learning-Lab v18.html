In-Place Quicksort

Move two pointers inward, swap out-of-place nodes inside the same array, then recursively sort the two resulting ranges.

Hoare Partition

Follow every step strictly. A wrong action repeats only the step where the error occurred.

  1. 1Choose the first-node or last-node pivot rule and confirm it once.
  2. 2For each recursive range, anchor the two pointers at the first searchable nodes beside the Pivot.
  3. 3Move the left pointer right while its value is smaller than the Pivot.
  4. 4Move the right pointer left while its value is larger than the Pivot.
  5. 5If the pointers have not crossed, swap their nodes. When they meet or cross: for a first-node Pivot, swap it with the right-pointer value (≤ Pivot); for a last-node Pivot, swap it with the left-pointer value (≥ Pivot).
  6. 6Fix the Pivot in place and exclude it. Recursively partition the left range first, followed by the right range.
  7. 7Reveal all values when every recursive range contains at most one node.
In-place rule: Nodes are swapped inside the original array. No auxiliary Smaller or Larger Arrays are created.
